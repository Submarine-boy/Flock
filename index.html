Good coffee. Good food. Good people.

Flock is an independent coffee house in Norwich offering well-crafted coffee, simple seasonal food and a welcoming atmosphere near St Stephens Square. We serve customers for dine-in, takeaway and delivery.

A little bit different.

Warm, cosy and friendly — Flock pairs thoughtful coffee and creative food with a relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere. Look out for the hip-hop records, Pac‑Man machine and small interior details that give the place personality.

  • Carefully prepared coffee and batch brews
  • Fresh pastries and homemade food
  • Community feeling and relaxed seating
